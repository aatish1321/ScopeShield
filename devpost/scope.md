# ScopeShield

Help freelancers turn extra client requests into reviewed, approved, paid additions to an existing agreement.

## The Unique Kernel
Connect AI's explanation of what changed in a client request directly to a freelancer-approved proposal and PayPal sandbox payment.

## Who It's For
A freelancer with a fixed-price agreement who receives additional client requests and needs to agree on compensation before doing the extra work.

## The Core Loop
Enter the original agreement and new request. AI identifies possible additions and explains its reasoning. The freelancer reviews and edits the proposed work, price, and delivery change. The client reviews the proposal, accepts, and pays using PayPal sandbox. Show the resulting payment status.

## Inspiration & Identity
ScopeShield is the accepted name. Visual direction is not established.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner chose this project for the PayPal + AI hackathon and wants the complete prototype built with concise communication rather than instruction.

## What "Working" Looks Like
Demonstrate a three-page website agreement followed by a request for a ten-product store. AI explains that the store is additional work. The freelancer sets and approves a change proposal. The client accepts and completes a PayPal sandbox payment, and ScopeShield shows confirmed payment status.

## The POC Boundary
One complete agreement-to-paid-change workflow, with real AI analysis, editable proposals, explicit human approval, and real PayPal sandbox integration. Include run instructions, a secret-free environment example, and an open source license for the submission.

## Later
Broader project management and pricing automation, if later requested.

## Explicitly Cut
- Automatic client charges: the accepted workflow requires client approval and payment.
- AI commitments about price or delivery without freelancer review: the freelancer owns these decisions.
- Full project management: unnecessary to prove the accepted core journey.
- Real-money payments: the supplied hackathon requires the free sandbox environment.

## Open Decisions
Exact API fit and provider configuration require verification before technical planning. Product details and visual direction remain for the product definition.
